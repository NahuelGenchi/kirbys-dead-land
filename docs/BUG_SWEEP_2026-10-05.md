# Self-directed bug sweep, 2026-10-05

After the Fire fix the user asked for an independent search for bugs. This
records what was checked, what was found and fixed, and what was investigated
and turned out to be correct.

## What was checked

- **Window shifts, headless.** Every copy ability (and base Kirby) ran two
  scripted plans on the original controller twice: once with the source window
  frozen, once moving by whole cells as in play. With a correct rebase Kirby's
  actions and every original object's place relative to him are identical.
  Plan A: chain climb and attack on the chain, walk, dash attack, tap, charge,
  up and down attacks, jump and air attack, float and exhale. Plan B: slide,
  guard, swimming in and out of water, attacks in water, the CPU helper. The
  stage locks the original camera vertically, so the frozen reference is valid
  only while Kirby stays low; floats beyond about 80 px are left out.
- **All 24 abilities in game.** One isolated lab run each (Kirby placed on the
  level's longest flat corridor; run, tap, charge, up, down, air and dash
  attacks, float and exhale, slide, guard; 30 fps capture), reviewed on contact
  sheets and by automatic per-frame checks: host and original health agree, the
  HUD agrees, Kirby's body is drawn while he is visible, his bitmap sits on the
  host hero, and the original and host positions agree.
- The same automatic checks over every earlier lab run with a trace (about 150).
- The logs of the user's own play sessions (no exceptions or mod errors).

## Fixed

**Kirby blinked out after every placement, and the original screen jumped at
every cell.** The original draws only its 256×192 screen, and its camera scrolls
at most 16 px a frame from its own scroll position (i16 at `0x0209F3A8`;
`0x0204222C` is recomputed from it each frame). `kssu_world` never moved that
position. When the host placed Kirby (start of every level, doors, teleporters,
pit respawns) he could land at screen x 376: he was not drawn for six frames,
about 0.1 s, and the camera took about 15 frames to catch up, culling effects on
one side. Every window cell also jumped his screen x by up to 24 px. The bridge
now moves the scroll with the window and frames Kirby at once after a placement
that leaves him more than 32 px off centre. Kirby is drawn from the first frame
of play (lab run `125144`); his screen x stays within 116–140 while running,
changing by at most 3 px a frame (before: 18 px jumps). `tests/speed` checks it.

**Kirby's feet slid at the Beheaded's speed.** The speed match moves him 1.91
times faster than the original walk and dash, but their cycles kept the original
rate, so he covered 78.8 px per walk cycle against the original's 41.3. The
original counts each pose down at `(player+0x40)+0x6C8`; the runtime now takes an
extra frame off that count in step with the matched speed (`AnimationPacer`),
playing the cycle up to 1.67 times faster (a pose cannot last less than one
frame): 47.3 px per cycle. `tests/speed` and the unit tests check it.

## Investigated, correct

- **Floating diverged between frozen and moving windows** (5 px at the top of a
  float): the frozen reference reached the top of the original's screen, where
  the original stops Kirby. In play the window keeps him near the middle.
- **Yellow Beam, green Plasma, cyan Ice Kirby**: the original's own per-ability
  body palettes, as an earlier review established.
- **Suplex Kirby climbing on a walkway**: down on a one-way walkway with a chain
  under it grabs the chain in both games; the host map stores walkways as one-way
  cells with chain cells below.
- **Helper nodes differing by whole cells**: their raw coordinates are equal in
  both runs (a fixed node at 0,0 and constants rewritten every frame), only their
  places relative to Kirby differ.
- **Paint's and Crash's screen effects**: fixed coordinates, not attached.
- **Stomp runs that landed below the zombie**: the host drops through a one-way
  platform when the dive starts within two cells of it, as the Beheaded does.
- **Fire's fireball dive and Crash's flash** drawing no body palette: original.

## Known, unchanged

- Detached original objects are drawn shifted by Kirby's movement during one
  frame (up to about 6 px at full run), since the frame is anchored on him.
- The climb's hand-over-hand cycle is driven by the original's own pulses, not a
  pose count, so it keeps the original tempo while Kirby climbs at the
  Beheaded's slower 97.5 px/s.
